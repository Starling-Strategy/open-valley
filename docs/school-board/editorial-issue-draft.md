## Three connected starting points

### 1. Define the core scenarios, then compare them on a map

- Today's observed seven-campus baseline.
- Retain the present configuration, explicitly modeling future services, enrollment and facilities investment.
- Selected existing-campus reconfiguration alternatives from the March 2025 report and Map Scenarios workbook. Verify and select individual options rather than treating them as one configuration.
- The later district FAQ concept: consolidated Valley elementary; Crossett Brook middle students to Harwood; Brookside students to Crossett Brook; Brookside closure. Verify against current board records.

This is an editorial comparison set, not an adopted board shortlist. Site choices, including the Cabot-property question, are variants within a configuration. Every option needs a source/version, school/grade assignments, comparable assumptions and explicit unknowns.

### 2. Explain a typical week at each school level

Build separate elementary, middle-school and high-school views from actual schedules. Cover academic time, specials/electives, support, staffing, peers, and relevant travel/after-school access. Preserve school differences, rotating schedule cycles and high-school pathway differences. Distinguish available services from time actually received and proposals from current provision.

### 3. Build a meeting-centered timeline, backward and forward

Board meetings are the units of collective work and decision-making. Summarize key past meetings using agendas, minutes and attachments: what was considered, decided or deferred, evidence presented, motions/outcomes, and follow-up. Identify committee recommendations separately from board actions.

Look ahead to officially scheduled meetings, posted agenda topics and anticipated decisions supported by records. Include participation details when published. An agenda establishes intended business, not an outcome or guaranteed vote. Show draft-minute status and last-checked dates.

## Supporting stories

Enrollment actuals versus forecasts; why budgets can rise while services are reduced; facilities needs and investment timing; community demographics and future pupils. Reuse sourced assets across web explainers, stills, meeting slides and short narrated videos.
