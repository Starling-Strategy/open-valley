## Register decision

Represent all six HUUSD communities: **Duxbury, Fayston, Moretown, Waitsfield, Warren, and Waterbury**. The official [district directory](https://huusd.org/our-schools) and [registration directory](https://huusd.org/student-enrollment) list seven schools/campuses. Preserve **eight K–12 reporting groups** when the enrollment source separates Harwood Union Middle School (HUMS) from Harwood Union High School (HUHS). Both belong to the same Harwood campus; a combined HUMHS total must not be added to its component groups.

The register describes **2026–27 as checked on October 4, 2026**. Undated school webpages establish the checked configuration, not the date on which a change took effect. Historical observations retain their own grade configurations. Current school/program facts and dated enrollment observations are separate records.

### Choice and program exceptions

- **K–8 intradistrict choice:** [HUUSD's current application page](https://huusd.org/apply-for-intradistrict-school-choice) explicitly permits applying to another district school regardless of resident town, subject to capacity and the procedures. This includes choosing either middle school for the grades it offers. Transportation is not guaranteed by the application page; the [FAQ](https://huusd.org/intradistrict-school-choice-faqs) describes buses to both middle schools from sending towns. Do not describe choice as automatic or promise transportation.
- **High-school choice and tuition:** E25 physical pp. 6 and 8 distinguish public high-school choice in/out, tuition in, and resident students. Town residence is therefore not equivalent to school attendance. Tuition-in students may be part of an attending total; tuition-out/resident counts are a different measure.
- **Harwood Community Learning Center (HCLC):** retain as an HUHS-linked program, not an eighth independent campus or an additional additive enrollment total. [Harwood Flexible Pathways](https://harwood.org/flexible-pathways) describes HCLC for juniors and seniors, combining Harwood classes, online learning, internships, and student-designed study. The [HCLC program page](https://www.harwood.org/hclc) verifies the alternative program and its origins in August 2000. A separately sourced physical address, separate current headcount, and the explicit rule for inclusion in HUHS totals remain unverified. Do not infer those from the school's generic contact footer.
- **Other high-school pathways:** the same official Flexible Pathways page lists Central Vermont Career Center, dual enrollment, Early College, internships, independent study, online learning, and travel study. They are options within the high-school experience, not additional HUUSD-operated schools. E25 explicitly includes dual-enrollment and technical-school participants in attending counts but excludes fully off-site Early College from its attending table. Preserve that distinction.
- **PK:** [HUUSD's 2026–27 PK page](https://huusd.org/prek) explicitly identifies **Warren, Moretown, and Brookside** as the three public hubs. Private Act 166 placements are not HUUSD-operated campuses. Keep public-hub attendance separate from district-funded public/private PK totals and from K–12.

## Dates, aliases, and material conflicts

1. **2016–17 predates operational unification.** The official [Articles of Agreement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1kLO__EbjGspdLndF3-_DeDhrJwZarjpG/view), revised 2018-10-24, physical pp. 5–6, establish full educational operations beginning **2017-07-01**. Use “predecessor WWSU schools serving today's HUUSD area” where appropriate; a district-ID change is not evidence of a campus closure. The November 2016 report supplies named-school coverage.
2. **Thatcher Brook → Brookside in 2021.** [Brookside About Us](https://bps.huusd.org/about-us) explicitly describes the rename and board approval in June 2021. Preserve Thatcher Brook Primary School / TBPS / TB and the older “Thatcher Brook Elementary” source wording as aliases of the school when the identity matches; do not introduce a discontinuity or double count.
3. **PK configuration changed.** Current Fayston and Waitsfield homepages say K–6 and the explicitly dated 2026–27 PK page lists only three hubs. The generic [Our Schools](https://huusd.org/our-schools) and [About HUUSD](https://huusd.org/about-huusd) pages still describe four PK–6 elementary schools. Prefer the current-year PK page plus individual school pages for the current configuration. Do not backdate that configuration across the ten-year history. Exact prior PK transition dates need year-specific evidence.
4. **Seven schools is compatible with eight reporting groups.** HUMHS is one combined school/location in the directory and AOE/VCGI PS138; district attendance tables can separate its middle/high groups. This is not evidence of an additional building.
5. **Plans are not completed reorganizations.** The older [bond FAQ](https://huusd.org/bond-plan-faqs) describes a proposed all-7/8-at-CBMS configuration. The current district directory, school sites, choice page, and 2025 enrollment tables retain HUMS and CBMS. Do not apply a proposed grade move to observed years.

## Current enrollment date/status

For **each of the eight K–12 groups**, the latest annual-report candidate discovered on the official enrollment page is **October 1, 2025** (E25). It is presented as the official annual observed report, not labeled preliminary; a separate final-certification statement was not found. The group's numerical value is **not extracted or approved in U1**.

Do not turn a September preliminary count, a forecast, or undated website phrases such as “over 600” into a current final observation.
