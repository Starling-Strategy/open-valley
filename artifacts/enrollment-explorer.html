Sources, methods and what the research found
  • Vermont Department of Health population tables: town workbook posted October 9, 2026, 1930–2025. Six town rows, columns 2000–2025. Annual estimates are not annual census counts. The 2011–19 values are revised intercensal estimates; census-year steps may reflect revisions rather than moves.
  • AOE enrollment workbook, November 25, 2025 edition: fall collection only. K full-time + K part-time + grades 1–12; excludes preschool and adult columns. This is a headcount sum, not weighted pupils or ADM. Repeated records with identical grade counts are counted once per school/year. Historical Harwood Middle (PS393) is included separately until the combined middle/high record replaces it. School IDs preserve the Thatcher Brook → Brookside name change.
  • District reports: reviewed October attending series, 2021–25, kept distinct from AOE. In fall 2024, AOE totals 1,559 versus 1,601 district attending; that 42-student difference remains unresolved. September 2026 is preliminary and appears below, not as an annual observation here.
  • Reviewed the repository’s enrollment coverage/source register and existing Enrollment and Demographic Research, and searched connected Vermont policy research. These point to age structure, housing and migration questions, not evidence that any one factor caused this trend.
  • MRV Planning District’s 2006 report offers older context, but its three-town Valley geography is narrower than this comparison. Its projections are not observed counts.
  • Coverage: population 2000–25; AOE enrollment 2003–04 to 2024–25, with a flagged gap; district attending through 2025–26. Extending enrollment to 2000 requires archived WWSU reports or NCES school-level records checked against the same roster.

What comes next

The missing piece for a real Sankey

An aggregate transition table—origin school/grade, destination school/grade or outside category, interval, and count—would distinguish students continuing, transferring within HUUSD, entering, or leaving.

We can already see

  • Enrollment at each grade and school.
  • Changes in expected cohorts.
  • Smaller entering classes and published forecasts.

We still need

  • October 2026 school-grade counts.
  • Actual aggregate school-to-school transitions.
  • Entry/exit timing and broad reasons.
  • Kindergarten origins and consistent program definitions.

No student identities are needed. Preserve suppression rules. PK, CTE, dual enrollment and Early College require explicit definitions to avoid double counting.

Source notes and known discrepancies

  • The email’s 1,586 baseline appears in the November and December 2025 workbook sheets. It is distinct from October attending and NESDEC headline counts.
  • The pasted workbook extraction contains malformed numeric labels. Only reconciled aggregates are used here; original cell/formula checks remain outstanding.
  • NESDEC reports 1,612 for 2023–24; repository review records district headline 1,605 and attending 1,597. Historical grade cells retain the consultant’s edition.
  • PK is excluded throughout. Public-campus PK and district-funded public/private PK are different populations, and PK spans multiple ages.
