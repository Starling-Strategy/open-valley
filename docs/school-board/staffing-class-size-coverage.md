## What the evidence can support

Keep four measures distinct: **teaching-position FTE**, **actual student learning-group/class size**, **dated class-size guidance**, and **usable capacity under explicit educational assumptions**. Enrollment divided by all teachers is a staffing ratio, not actual class size. A count of physical rooms does not establish how many children can be taught in the intended groups.

## Class-size guidance is dated policy, not a measurement

1. **Local choice-capacity procedure.** The district's current [choice page](https://huusd.org/apply-for-intradistrict-school-choice) links [F33-L procedures](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1chnym4ainhdQCc4JgndG_AEpmZAdgzXEWdGrfJsFarI/edit), adopted by the administrative team 2018-05-14, revised 2019-11-25 and **2022-11-28** in the checked original. Its capacity paragraph says a request may be denied if it expands a class to **18 students per teacher in K–3 or 23 in 4–8**, and also considers class composition/needs. This is a **choice-admission threshold**, not a measured average or an unconditional class-size maximum. It references **HUUSD G-14, Class Size**. The current adopted G-14 edition and any subsequent amendment were **not located/verified** in this pass; that is a precise follow-on gap.
2. **State minimum standards.** [16 V.S.A. §165(a)(9)](https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/section/16/003/00165), checked on the legislature's online statute page, marks the provision effective **2026-07-01**. It lists average minimums of 10 for grade 1, 12 for grades 2–5, 15 for grades 6–8 required content areas, and 18 for grades 9–12 required content areas. It distinguishes an average from an individual class and lists exemptions, multiage restrictions, occupancy limits and waivers. These are not target class sizes or evidence that HUUSD is meeting/failing them. Check the applicable current rules and amendments when doing the follow-on analysis; the webpage identifies itself as an unofficial statutory compilation including 2025-session actions.
3. **AOE working definitions.** [Official landing page](https://education.vermont.gov/documents/edu-sbe-class-size-min-working-definitions-memo), posted 2026-05-27, links the [six-page memo](https://education.vermont.gov/sites/aoe/files/documents/edu-sbe-class-size-min-working-definitions-memo.pdf) **dated February 6, 2026** internally. Do not mistake the webpage date for the memo's vintage. p. 2 defines school/year/content-area averages and multiage treatment; pp. 3–5 discuss exclusions; p. 6 says the first intended collection is spring 2027/reporting fall 2027, with a planned 2025–26 baseline for districts in fall 2026. U1 verified that publication and wording, **not that a HUUSD baseline has been released**. The memo explicitly says working definitions do not replace State Board rulemaking. SHA-256 `b10058ecaa251188a5fb3395e29b733fd305998ae5fb29da6df5fa04730cca73`.

The F33-L plain-text export checked in memory has SHA-256 `c5fd23d0a0e1eb89e8d44fa5de97159b826e0522325a203a10123ac048dcc8db`. The live linked version may change; use the dated reviewed edition when interpreting past decisions.

## Capacity definition found in the original

P25 physical p. 6 calculates elementary/middle capacity from the smaller of a classroom-area allowance and the report's stated classroom limits: **50 square feet per student for kindergarten and middle-school science labs, 35 for other classrooms; stated limits 20 for K–4 and 25 for 5–8**. These are **the consultant report's assumptions**. The report itself says the resulting figures are higher than HUUSD's optimal class size, that space use could change, and that further study is needed. Do not present its characterization of state limits as independently verified current law, especially alongside the differently framed 2026 standards.

Consequently, a room-derived capacity total must not be relabeled as useful teaching capacity or compared with PK-inclusive district enrollment. A follow-on analysis needs the occupied/program room uses, dated grade bands, actual group sizes, staffing and scheduling constraints, and explicit assumptions. Preserve the consultant result as a dated modeled quantity, distinct from observed classes or enrollment. Scenario selection remains outside this release.
